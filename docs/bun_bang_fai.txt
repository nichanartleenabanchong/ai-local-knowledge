BUN BANG FAI (Rocket Festival) | ประเพณีบุญบั้งไฟ ยโสธร

Overview
Bun Bang Fai is a rocket festival of the Northeast (Isan) of Thailand and Laos. "Bang fai" means home-made bamboo rocket. The most famous Bun Bang Fai is held in Yasothon province.

Dates
It is usually held in May (around the 6th lunar month), at the start of the rainy season, before farmers plant rice. In Yasothon it is usually held on the second weekend of May.

History and beliefs
- Villagers launch rockets into the sky to remind the rain god, Phaya Thaen, to send rain for the rice fields.
- A local legend tells of Phaya Khankhak, a toad king who fought Phaya Thaen. After Phaya Thaen lost, he agreed to send rain when people launched rockets as a signal.
- The festival is connected to the "Heet Sip Song" (the twelve monthly traditions) of Isan culture.

Rituals and customs
- Villages make big rockets from bamboo or PVC pipe filled with black powder. Rockets compete to see which one flies highest and longest.
- Parades with decorated rockets, dancers and music (such as the phin and khaen).
- Teams whose rocket fails to launch are often thrown into the mud as a fun punishment.
- There is a lot of humor and folk play during the parade.
